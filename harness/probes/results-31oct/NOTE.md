# The three drawings from 31 October

`probe-31oct.json` records the three corrected steps Muse Image drew on 31 October 2026 (`muse_image`, with their cost).
The image files were damaged when they were committed: git stripped the CR from every CR LF byte pair, so they no
longer decode. They were drawn again on 3 November with the same learners, question and code
(`../redraw_3nov.escript`), and the new files and their costs are in `../results-03nov/`. See `evidence/MEDIA-REPAIR.md`.
